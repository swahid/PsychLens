## 1. Standard Privacy Policy

PsychLens is architected on modern **Privacy-by-Design** and **Data Minimization** principles:

### 1.1 Non-Biometric Processing Safe Harbor (GDPR Art. 9 & CCPA)
* PsychLens does **NOT** generate, calculate, record, extract, or retain mathematical biometric templates, facial geometry coordinates, vector embeddings of personal features, or identity templates.
* Processing is transient and ephemeral, evaluating general semantic attributes (e.g. ambient color psychology, contextual posture, lighting, and perceived mood).

### 1.2 Client-Side Data Scrubbing (Zero Metadata Transmission)
* Prior to processing, every photograph is downscaled and re-encoded client-side using HTML5 Canvas into WebP format (~80KB).
* All private EXIF metadata—including GPS geographic coordinates, device hardware serial numbers, camera model details, and timestamps—is stripped locally before any network request occurs.

### 1.3 Ephemeral Multi-Node Storage (≤7 Days Auto-Purge)
* For free users who voluntarily share moments to the public feed, photos are routed through ephemeral nodes (ImgBB / Litterbox) configured with automatic Time-to-Live (TTL) deletion in **7 days or less**.
* Images naturally vanish without persistent long-term storage footprints.

### 1.4 Minimal Google Photos Scope (`appendonly`)
* For subscribers who choose to synchronize their vault to Google Photos, authentication is restricted to the minimal `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/photoslibrary.appendonly` scope.
* This permission strictly permits creating and adding to the user's dedicated *"PsychLens Private Vault"* album. PsychLens cannot read, view, modify, or delete any of your other existing Google Photos.

### 1.5 Right to be Forgotten (GDPR Art. 17 & Apple Guideline 5.1.1v)
* Users have permanent access to the in-app **"Clear Vault & Reset"** trigger, which immediately wipes all local IndexedDB records, cached images, session identifiers, and cookies in one tap.

## 2. Content Policy (PG-13 / PG-16)

PsychLens is designed as a family-safe, reflective visual perception tool. It is strictly rated **PG-13 / PG-16**.

### 2.1 Zero-Tolerance Prohibitions
The following categories of content are strictly prohibited from capture, upload, analysis, or broadcast:
* **Adult, Pornographic or Sexually Explicit Media**: Nudity, sexual acts, suggestive posing, or exploitation.
* **Graphic Violence & Gore**: Bodily mutilation, physical abuse, severe injury, or blood.
* **Hate Speech & Harassment**: Imagery or messaging that promotes discrimination, disparagement, racism, or hostility based on race, ethnicity, religion, disability, age, nationality, sexual orientation, or gender identity.
* **Self-Harm & Suicide**: Depictions or encouragement of self-directed injury.
* **Non-Consensual Imagery**: Unauthorized capture violating reasonable expectations of personal privacy.

### 2.2 Automated Safety Screening Gate
* Every uploaded or captured image is screened by automated safety filters prior to running visual analysis.
* Prohibited content is rejected immediately with a clear explanation.
* **Credit Protection**: Users are never charged credits for blocked or rejected images. Repeat violations trigger permanent account suspension.
